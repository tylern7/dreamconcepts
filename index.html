The details

Everything we offer

Landscaping construction

A landscape adds so much more than good looks to your home. Dream Concepts specializes in landscaping, snow removal and renovation services, and our trained landscapers can care for your grounds year-round.

We do decks, fences, gardens, interlocks, whole garden designs, walkways, pathways, driveways, patios, wood and stone retaining walls, waterfalls and ponds. Call for a free estimate: we'll go over your requirements, make suggestions and work with you to make your dream landscape a reality.

Landscape & garden maintenance

Our landscapers use professionally inspected, certified tools to trim, weed and nurture the trees and shrubs around your home. Our staff can look after expensive and exotic plants and help prepare your landscape for winter.

Home renovations

Kitchens: renovations that don't break the bank, making the room more functional and good-looking.

Bathrooms: modern designs with designer lights and fittings, a jacuzzi or a wider shower. You decide it all.

Bedrooms & living rooms: modernize to your taste, let in more light by removing a wall, install larger windows or improve insulation.

Basements: right up to an entire fully finished basement. Plus general contracting, remodelling and repairs down to your chipped bathroom tile.

Snow removal

Snow clearing: driveways, sidewalks, stairs and porches, cleared with certified tools and machinery.

Snow salting: melts ice to help prevent slips and falls.

Regular snow removal: packages built around your property and budget, with a team working around the clock in heavy snowfall.

Why hire a landscaper instead of DIY?

Big projects take time, investment and care, and DIY jobs often end up half-finished. Hiring Dream Concepts means the job is done right, you have a knowledgeable team on call, and the job actually gets finished. Professional landscaping can also raise your home's selling price.
